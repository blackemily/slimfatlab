Panax Ginseng and Eleuthero

Panax ginseng and eleuthero are different plants, although both are sometimes discussed in connection with energy, fatigue, or traditional herbal practices. They should not be treated as interchangeable. Research findings for one species or extract do not automatically apply to another.

Studies of ginseng have explored several possible effects, but results vary by preparation, dose, study population, and outcome measured. Evidence about fatigue or other areas is not equivalent to evidence that a supplement causes weight loss. Eleuthero also has a different research history and should be evaluated separately.

These botanicals may interact with medicines or be unsuitable for some people. Ginseng, for example, may affect blood sugar or interact with anticoagulant and other medications. People with chronic conditions or taking prescription drugs should review the exact product label with a clinician or pharmacist before use.
