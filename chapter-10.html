Ashwagandha: Stress, Sleep, and Caution

Ashwagandha is an herb that has been studied in relation to stress and sleep, among other topics. Some trials have reported changes in perceived stress or sleep measures, but the findings depend on the extract, dose, duration, and participants. Those findings do not establish that ashwagandha causes weight loss or that the amount in Nagano produces the same results.

Stress and sleep can influence eating patterns and daily routines. Someone who sleeps poorly may find it harder to plan meals, stay active, or manage cravings. That does not mean an herb is a substitute for addressing the underlying causes of stress or sleep problems.

Ashwagandha is not appropriate for everyone. It has been associated with rare liver injury and may affect thyroid function; it can also interact with sedatives, immune-related medicines, and other treatments. People who are pregnant, have thyroid or liver conditions, or take regular medication should seek professional advice before using it.
