A clear path, not a scattered collection of topics.

There is no single explanation for why people gain weight, why losing it can be difficult, or why keeping it off takes continued effort. Food choices matter, but so do appetite, sleep, stress, activity, health conditions, medications, environment, and the habits built around everyday life.

Slim Fat Lab is organized as a step-by-step learning journey. First understand what influences body weight. Then build the habits that help you respond to those influences. Finally, put the pieces together into a realistic approach you can sustain.

Our approach: practical education, realistic expectations, and careful evaluation of popular claims. This guide is educational and is not a substitute for individualized medical advice.
