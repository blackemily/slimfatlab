Safety, Medication Interactions, and Who Should Ask First

Natural does not automatically mean harmless. Botanical ingredients can have pharmacological effects, interact with medicines, or be inappropriate for particular health conditions. A multi-ingredient formula creates an additional challenge because several ingredients may have overlapping effects or different cautions.

People taking medication for blood pressure, diabetes, blood clotting, thyroid conditions, immune conditions, or sleep should review the complete ingredient list with a healthcare professional or pharmacist. People with liver or kidney disease, those preparing for surgery, and anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding should also seek advice before using a multi-ingredient supplement.

Stop using a supplement and seek medical advice if you develop concerning symptoms such as yellowing of the skin or eyes, dark urine, severe abdominal pain, unusual bleeding, fainting, or signs of an allergic reaction. Keep the container so the exact product and lot information are available.
