Green Tea Extract and EGCG

Green tea is widely consumed as a beverage and contains naturally occurring compounds called catechins. One of the best-known is epigallocatechin gallate, commonly abbreviated EGCG. Green tea extracts may concentrate these compounds, which means a supplement dose can differ substantially from the amount someone would receive from an ordinary cup of tea.

Research on green tea components and body weight has produced mixed and generally modest findings. Results from studies of a particular standardized extract cannot automatically be applied to Nagano Lean Belly Tonic. The finished product would need to be studied in its own right, using its actual formula and serving instructions, before product-specific effectiveness could be established.

Concentrated green tea extracts also deserve caution. Some products have been associated with liver-related adverse effects, particularly at high doses or when taken inappropriately. Anyone with liver disease, unexplained symptoms, or concerns about medications should discuss concentrated extracts with a qualified healthcare professional. More is not necessarily better.
